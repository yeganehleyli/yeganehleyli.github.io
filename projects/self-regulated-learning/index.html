The learning challenge

Learning to write also involves learning how to plan, use feedback, and reflect. This project integrated self-regulated learning into a flipped-learning experience, using EdPuzzle, Google Docs, and Padlet to structure those activities.

My contribution

  • Contributed to instructional design and the sequence of digital learning activities.
  • Designed around goal-setting, feedback, and reflection.
  • Used data-informed evaluation to examine the learning experience.

Reported result

+1.92 points

Higher final writing score after accounting for initial ability.

For the study design, comparison-group results, and measurement details, read the expanded Writing with intention case study.

What the work brings together

The project demonstrates how the choice and sequence of digital activities can support learner agency. The tools serve a purposeful learning process that gives learners opportunities to set goals, receive feedback, and reflect.
