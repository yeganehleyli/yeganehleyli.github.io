Meta-MILE

The Future of Education: A Multi-Layered Metaverse Classroom Model for Immersive and Inclusive Learning

An inclusive framework for learning in virtual environments, connecting emerging technology with human needs.

The learning challenge

How can emerging virtual environments become spaces for inclusive learning? Meta-MILE explores this question through a multi-layered metaverse classroom model. The project connects the possibilities of immersive technology with instructional design and the needs of learners.

My contribution

  • Contributed to the conceptual design of the learning framework.
  • Wrote and edited research content to make the model clear and coherent.
  • Used visualization to communicate the framework and collaborated across disciplines.

Reported result

Inclusive by design

A framework for immersive, inclusive learning in virtual environments.

This project presents a conceptual framework. The portfolio does not report a learner-outcome evaluation for Meta-MILE.

What the work brings together

This work brings together conceptual thinking, communication, and instructional design. It demonstrates my approach to making emerging technology understandable and relevant to a learning experience.
