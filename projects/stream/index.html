The learning challenge

Recorded STEM lessons contain valuable information, but learners also need clear guidance through that information. STREAM explores an AI-supported workflow that transforms recorded lessons into concise, sequenced, and traceable visual guidance.

My contribution

  • Contributed to the design of an AI-supported instructional workflow.
  • Wrote and edited content with attention to sequence and clarity.
  • Brought accessibility thinking and quality review to the learning materials.

Reported result

9.2 / 10

Average content-quality rubric score in the offline feasibility pilot.

This is a pilot content-quality result. It is not a measure of student learning gains or evidence of effectiveness at scale.

What the work brings together

The project connects instructional judgment with AI-supported content transformation. My contribution focused on how information is organized, communicated, and reviewed so the result can serve a learner’s needs.
