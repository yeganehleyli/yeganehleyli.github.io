The learning challenge

Trying a new technology can be as much about comfort and usability as it is about features. This study explored a 20-minute VR design-studio experience with computer-design students, considering usability, comfort, and perceived relevance to career readiness.

My contribution

  • Contributed to the study design for the brief virtual design-studio experience.
  • Analyzed the student experience with attention to usability, comfort, and perceived relevance.
  • Developed recommendations for training and support.

Reported result

16 of 28

Students reported increased comfort with the technology.

The result describes self-reported comfort after a brief experience. It does not establish long-term learning or career-readiness outcomes.

What the work brings together

The work highlights the value of listening to learners as they encounter an unfamiliar environment. Research on their experience can inform the guidance, preparation, and support that surround the technology.
